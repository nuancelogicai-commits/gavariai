About Gavari AI

An AI studio built for
what's next.

Gavari AI LLC is an AI app development and digital strategy firm. We build products that put artificial intelligence to work on problems that matter — for our clients and for the market.

Who We Are

Gavari AI LLC is a Wyoming-based AI application development and digital strategy company. We specialize in building consumer and enterprise-grade AI products from the ground up — handling everything from product architecture and backend infrastructure to growth systems and go-to-market strategy.

We operate as a lean, AI-native studio. Every product we touch is built with artificial intelligence at its core — not as a feature, but as the foundation.

Our flagship product, Authentica, is an AI-powered authentication app for sneakers and apparel. It's a direct demonstration of the kind of focused, high-quality product development we bring to every engagement.

What We Believe

We believe the best AI products are the ones that solve real problems simply. Not technology for its own sake — AI deployed precisely where it creates genuine value for the person using it.

We believe speed and quality aren't opposites. Moving fast with intention, clear architecture, and a strong product foundation produces better outcomes than slow and over-engineered.

We believe distribution is as important as the product itself. A great app that nobody knows about isn't a business. We build the growth infrastructure alongside the product — so both are ready on launch day.

Gregory St. Clair

Founder & Managing Member

Gregory St. Clair is the founder and Managing Member of Gavari AI LLC. He leads product strategy, business development, marketing, and growth across all of Gavari AI's products and client engagements.

With a background in AI digital strategy and a focus on building systems that scale, Gregory co-founded Authentica — an AI-powered authentication app built to solve the $1 trillion counterfeit goods problem — and leads its commercial strategy from product conception through App Store launch and beyond.

Gavari AI's approach reflects Gregory's operating philosophy: move fast, build with intention, and create products that solve problems people actually have.
